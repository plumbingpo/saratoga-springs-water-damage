Roof Leak Water Damage in Saratoga Springs: What to Know

The spot on your ceiling is seldom directly below the hole in the roof. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

Your First Steps

  1. Cut Power to Wet FixturesWater coming through a ceiling fan, a light or close to an outlet means that circuit should be turned off at the panel, and the fixture should not be touched.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Drain a Bulging CeilingA ceiling that sags with trapped water can be drained: stay off to the side and make a small opening at its lowest spot, with a bucket underneath.
  4. Stay Off the RoofA wet roof and an unlit attic are both places to stay out of. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • A valley liner that has worn through at the point where the most runoff passes.
  • Gutters packed with leaves, which push water back beneath the first row of shingles.
  • Sun-cracked rubber collars where plumbing vent pipes come through the roof.
  • Flashing that has rusted, lifted or pulled away around chimneys, vents and walls.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
If It Is Left

What Happens if You Wait

Hidden Attic Mold

Damp framing, paper-faced drywall, warmth and darkness give mold what it needs. It may take hold in 24 to 48 hours and work its way over the underside of the sheathing.

Wall Cavities

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. You might notice nothing more than a peeling patch or a puffy baseboard.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Drywall Ceilings

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. Joints open, tape peels, and a waterlogged piece may fall without much notice.

How We Fix It

Our Roof Leak Water Damage Process

1

Map Where Water Went

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Cover and Protect

If the roof is still open to the weather, we can install a temporary tarp or coordinate with your roofer. We also cover floors and contents under the damaged ceiling.

We pull out insulation and drywall that cannot be saved, then run air movers and dehumidifiers in the attic and the rooms below, checking moisture readings until the framing is dry.

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. After the roofer has finished, new insulation goes in, fresh drywall is hung and finished, and the ceiling is primed and painted to match.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

When a leak happens suddenly and by accident, for example a branch going through the roof or wind stripping shingles, many homeowners policies pay for the water damage inside. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.
