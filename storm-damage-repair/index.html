Our Storm Damage Repair Process

Our systematic approach delivers quality results on every project. Here's how we handle storm damage repair from start to finish:

1

Emergency Board-Up & Tarping

We secure your property by tarping roof damage, boarding broken windows and sealing breaches so no more water gets in while permanent repairs are planned.

2

Extract Water and Assess Damage

Our team pumps and vacuums out the water that came in with the storm. We then assess all affected areas with moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of the damage.

3

Structural Drying

To keep mold from growing, we dry the flooring, wall cavities, insulation and roof decking with commercial air movers and dehumidifiers.

4

Permanent Repairs & Reconstruction

Damaged roofing, siding, drywall, insulation, flooring and other components are repaired or replaced. Your property is restored to its pre-storm condition.

How to Prevent Storm Damage Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Cut back limbs that hang over or close to the house so wind cannot throw them into it
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Make sure the attic is properly ventilated and insulated to prevent ice dams
  • Before storm season each year, get the roof inspected and its problem areas repaired

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Saratoga Springs

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • After a weather event, insulation inside walls or overhead feels soaked
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • A basement or ground floor that floods when the drains cannot keep up
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
